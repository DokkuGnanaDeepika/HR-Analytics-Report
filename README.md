# HR-Analytics-Report
The HR Analytics Report is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to help and leadership analyze workforce dynamics, track key operational metrics . The report evaluates active employee headcounts, compensation structures, attrition trends, overtime distribution, employee performance, and work-life balance scores across various departments.




## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding workforce trends is critical for talent retention, compensation planning, and operational efficiency. This dashboard consolidates key HR metrics to help decision-makers:
- **Monitor Active Headcount & Trends:** Track real-time employee counts and monthly hiring/headcount fluctuations.
- **Analyze Compensation & Costs:** Measure total compensation costs, department salary averages, and role-based overtime hour distributions.
- **Evaluate Attrition & Wellness:** Track overall attrition rates against targets while evaluating employee work-life balance and performance ratings.
- **Assess Training ROI:** Analyze the relationship between training hours and performance ratings.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Highlights

* **Active Employees:** 1,389 unique employees actively working across 8 core departments.
* **Total Compensation:** ₹140.00M in total base salary and bonus payouts.
* **Attrition Rate:** 41.12% turnover rate evaluated against key organizational benchmarks.
* **Average Performance Rating:** 3.46 across active staff.
* **Overtime Tracking:** Over 30,500 total overtime hours logged across key job functions (Analysts, Associates, Managers, Executives, Senior Engineers).

## 📊 Dashboard Breakdown

The Power BI report includes multiple dedicated analysis pages:

1. **Overview Dashboard**
   - High-level KPIs: Active Employees, Total Compensation, Attrition Rate, Average Rating.
   - Monthly active employee trends.
   - Active headcount breakdown by department (IT leading with 163, Finance at 122).
   - Global interactive filters by *Department Name*, *Start of Month*, *Attrition Flag*, *Job Role*, and *Gender*.

2. **Workforce Cost Page**
   - Average monthly salary comparison by department (HR leads at ₹74K, Finance/IT around ₹70K-₹72K).
   - Treemap breakdown of Total Compensation by Job Role (Analyst: ₹29.70M, Manager: ₹29.28M, Associate: ₹28.30M).
   - Detailed breakdown of total overtime hours by Job Role.

3. **Performance & Wellness Page**
   - Matrix of performance scores across Job Roles and Departments.
   - Scatter plot correlating **Training Hours** vs. **Performance Rating**.
   - Attrition gauge visual displaying turnover against key target thresholds.
   - Work-Life Balance scores across departments (Customer Support highest at 3.00, HR lowest at 2.78).

## 🛠️ Data & Tools Used

- **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Data Source:** HR Information System (HRIS) dataset containing demographic, salary, overtime, training, and performance metrics.
- **Key DAX Measures:** Headcount calculations, Attrition Rate percentages, Average Satisfaction Scores, and Compensation aggregations.

---

## 🚀 How to Use / View

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the top slicers/filters on the **Overview** page to slice data by department, gender, role, or date range.
4. Navigate between tabs using the bottom navigation panel (*Workforce Cost*, *Performance Wellness*, *Job Satisfaction*, etc.).
